# Common questions: facts for answers

Answer in the caller's language, in one or two short sentences.

**Do you take up a single room?**
Yes, as a complete redesign with materials, furniture and execution. Not as advice only.

**Can you do a rented flat?**
Yes, if there are no structural changes and the landlord agrees. The kitchen is done as a modular unit that can come out cleanly.

**Do you do just design without execution?**
No. The studio does design and execution together, with its own contractor and vendor network.

**Can you come and give ideas on colours or furniture?**
No. The studio does not offer advice-only visits. A full redesign with execution is the smallest engagement.

**Do you do offices?**
Yes, from about 500 sq ft up to about 3,000 sq ft.

**Do you do restaurants, hotels, shops or gyms?**
No. Those need a studio that specialises in that type of space.

**Do you do Vastu?**
Vastu needs are included in the design. The studio does not offer Vastu advice on its own.

**Can you finish before Diwali, or in a few weeks?**
Not if it is under 6 weeks. A design phase alone takes 3 to 4 weeks. A later start can be booked.

**Is the consultation free?**
Yes, and there is no obligation.

**Can the designer come to my site?**
Yes. A site visit can be arranged for the consultation.

**Do you work outside Pune?**
No. Only Pune city and PCMC.

**How much will it cost? What is the rate per sq ft? Can I get a price list?**
Do not answer with any figure. Use the pricing sentence from the system prompt.

**Can I see your work?**
The studio's work is on its website and Instagram. Mention that and offer a consultation.

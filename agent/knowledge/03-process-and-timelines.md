# Process and timelines

## Steps
1. Free consultation. At the studio or a visit to the site. No obligation.
2. Design phase: 3 to 4 weeks from the first consultation.
3. Execution: 8 to 16 weeks, depending on project size and how ready the site is.

## Lead time
Execution cannot begin on a project that needs to be ready in under 6 weeks from today. A single-room redesign with execution takes at least 8 to 10 weeks from start to finish.

If someone needs a result sooner, the honest answer is that the studio cannot do it justice. A later start can be offered.

## The consultation
- It is free and carries no obligation.
- The designer sees the site, or the plan, and discusses the scope, materials and the way forward.
- The designer already has the caller's details from this call, so they will not be asked the same questions again.
- Pricing is discussed in detail at the consultation, not on the phone.
- Where possible, everyone who will decide about the project should attend.

## Possession and new properties
Customers can book a consultation before possession if the builder allows access to the site. They do not need to wait.

# Aangan Studio: services

Facts only. Rules for how to behave live in the system prompt.

## What we do
End-to-end interior design for homes and small offices in Pune and PCMC:
- Space planning and layout
- Material selection: flooring, wall finishes, ceilings
- Furniture design and curation, custom and sourced
- Lighting design
- Kitchen and wardrobe design
- Execution supervision, using the studio's own network of contractors and vendors

## Residential
Apartments, independent houses and villas. This can be a full home, a full floor, or individual rooms, where the job is a complete redesign with execution.

## Commercial
Offices, clinics and studios, from about 500 sq ft up to about 3,000 sq ft. TODO for Nikhil: confirm the 500 sq ft minimum (it was said on a call but is not in the services guide). Offices include workstations, cabins, reception and common areas.

## What we do not do
- Architecture or structural work. We do not move walls, change the building structure, or handle permits for structural changes.
- Decor or styling advice only, such as colour suggestions or rearranging furniture.
- Standalone furniture sourcing without a design project.
- Standalone Vastu advice. We include Vastu needs in our designs.
- Restaurants, hotels, retail stores and gyms.
- Projects outside Pune city and PCMC.

## Typical project sizes
- Full home: 2BHK and above, design and execution.
- Partial home: a full floor, or two or more rooms with execution.
- Single room: a bedroom or living room, as a complete redesign with materials, furniture and execution. Not advice only.
- Office: up to about 3,000 sq ft.
- Rented flats are fine when there is no structural change. Kitchens in rented flats are done as modular units that can be removed cleanly.
